FileSync Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026 / First published: May 3, 2026

Summary: FileSync does not use advertising or tracking. File transfers go directly to your chosen service, and credentials are used to authenticate those connections. Credentials use OS secure storage; metadata, settings, and history are also stored in app-private storage. Automatic external diagnostic reporting is disabled in Android 1.0.3 (29885088 / DeployGate #166) and subsequent builds containing the same change. Section 2.2 distinguishes older versions, local logs, and manual sharing.

1. Introduction

FileSync ("the App") treats user privacy as its highest priority. This Privacy Policy describes what information the App handles, and how. The App is a file manager that integrates Google Drive, Dropbox, WebDAV-compatible storage (Koofr, pCloud, and others), SFTP / FTP servers, USB storage, and on-device local storage in a single interface.

2. Information Handled

2.1 Information not collected for advertising or tracking

The following are not collected for advertising or tracking. Version-specific diagnostic handling is described below:

  • Personally identifiable information (name, address, phone number, email address, etc.)
  • The contents of files you operate on
  • Location data
  • Advertising identifiers (IDFA / GAID)
  • Usage history or behavioral analytics for advertising purposes

The App does not use third-party analytics platforms for advertising or behavioral tracking purposes (Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, Amplitude, etc.).

2.2 Crash Reports and Diagnostic Logs

Android 1.0.3 (build 29885088 / DeployGate #166) and subsequent builds containing the same reporting-disable change do not automatically send crash reports, diagnostic logs, usage statistics, or performance information externally. On iOS, this applies to distributed builds containing that change; it does not automatically change older distributed builds.

Older versions, such as DeployGate #165, may automatically report error details, device / OS / app versions, timestamps, and stack traces to Sentry. Masking in older versions does not guarantee anonymization of every file name or path. Updating the App or deleting local data does not delete diagnostic information already sent.

Diagnostic logs are also stored on the device. Exporting logs and automatically transmitting them are separate operations. If you manually share a log, review its contents and recipient first. Diagnostics and access logs collected independently by the OS or your chosen services follow their respective settings and policies.

2.3 Information stored on your device

The following information is stored on your device. Necessary credentials are sent to your chosen service when connecting. Local diagnostic logs and reporting by older versions are described above.

  • OAuth access tokens / refresh tokens for each cloud service
  • Connection details and credentials (username, password, SSH key) for WebDAV / SFTP / FTP
  • Temporary cache of file listings (metadata)
  • Sync settings, view preferences, and favorites
  • History of backup and transfer jobs, and local diagnostic logs

Credentials use iOS Keychain or Android secure storage. Metadata, settings, and history are also stored in databases and other app-private storage isolated by the OS; the App does not separately encrypt all such data.

2.4 Information transmitted externally by user action

When you use backup, upload, or transfer features, target files are sent to the destinations you specify (cloud storage services, WebDAV / SFTP / FTP servers, etc.). These are direct transmissions to services you have explicitly chosen; they do not pass through the developer's servers.

3. Third-Party Services

The App communicates under your account with the services you choose to connect. The developer only acts as a client; credentials are used to authenticate your chosen connections. Reporting by older versions is described above.

  • Google Drive (Google LLC Privacy Policy)
  • Dropbox (Dropbox Privacy Policy)
  • pCloud (pCloud Privacy Policy)
  • WebDAV / SFTP / FTP servers (any server you specify)

Data handling at these services is governed by each provider's privacy policy. Older versions may use Sentry for diagnostic reporting. See section 2.2 for the applicable builds and reporting-disable scope.

4. OS Permissions

The following permissions support these features. Request timing depends on the OS, app initialization, and feature settings:

  • Photo library: for automatic photo/video backup
  • Local network: for WebDAV / SFTP / FTP server connections
  • Face ID / Touch ID / biometrics: for the app lock
  • File sharing / document browser: for file import/export
  • Background processing: to perform background transfers when permitted by the OS; continuous transfer after the app is closed is not guaranteed
  • External storage (Android): to access USB / SD card content

5. Cookies and Tracking

The App does not use cookies. None of the following are present:

  • Collection of advertising identifiers (IDFA / GAID)
  • Cross-site or cross-app tracking
  • Connections to third-party ad networks
  • Tracking subject to App Tracking Transparency (ATT)

6. Security

  • Credentials and tokens use OS secure storage
  • Encryption depends on the connection type and settings. HTTPS and SFTP are encrypted; plain FTP and HTTP connections are not. Choose an encrypted connection for sensitive information
  • The app supports a biometric app lock (Face ID / Touch ID / fingerprint)
  • SFTP supports public-key authentication

7. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. International Data Transfers

File contents do not pass through the developer's servers. Your chosen services may store data outside your country. Diagnostics already sent to Sentry by older versions may also be stored abroad. See each service's privacy policy for details.

9. GDPR / CCPA

For questions about data handling or deletion, please use the contact information below. Deletion of device credentials and uploaded files is described in the next section.

10. Data Deletion

10.1 Credentials and connection settings

  • Logging out / disconnecting in each service's screen removes the corresponding tokens from the device
  • Credentials may remain in iOS Keychain after uninstalling. Disconnect your services before uninstalling
  • You can also revoke app access in the service's authorization settings. Deleting device data does not delete diagnostic information already sent

10.2 Files already uploaded

Delete them directly from each cloud / server's management UI or official app. The App also supports delete operations.

11. Changes to This Policy

Material changes will be announced via in-app notification, App Store / Google Play release notes, and an update to the "Last updated" date on this page.

12. Contact

For privacy-related questions, please file an issue on the App's GitHub repository.

  • Contact: GitHub Issues
  • Operator: FileSync development team (independent developer)

13. Summary

FileSync is built on the principles of "local-first" and "privacy by design":

  • Credentials authenticate your chosen connections; section 2.2 identifies builds with automatic diagnostic reporting disabled
  • File contents do not pass through the developer's servers
  • Credentials are protected in secure storage on the device
  • Data is not sold. Transmissions to your chosen services, local logs, and diagnostic reporting by older versions are described above
  • No ads or tracking
